PRIVACY CONTROLS

Cookies & device storage

Bookable263 uses the minimum browser storage needed to keep your account secure.

Essential sign-in cookie

When you sign in, Bookable263 sets an HTTP-only session cookie named b263_session. The browser cannot read it from page scripts. It uses the same-site strict setting and the secure flag when the site runs in production over HTTPS.

Regular sign-in lasts 12 hours and ends when you close the browser. If you select “Remember me”, the session lasts 30 days. New account registration starts with a 7-day session. You can end a session any time by signing out.

No advertising or analytics cookies

This version does not set advertising, cross-site tracking or analytics cookies. A browser-local preference records whether you have dismissed the cookie notice; it is not used to identify you across sites.

Change this device's notice preference

You can show the cookie notice again on this device. This does not sign you out or change the essential session cookie.

Cookie settings are informational because only essential sign-in cookies are currently used.
